Host partitions

Scan every OS-visible disk — HBA, JBOD, NVMe, md arrays, and RAID logical drives — and clear leftover partition tables (including ESP) so old OS/EFI volumes cannot compete after Deploy. This zaps GPT/MBR signatures — it does not zero the whole disk. The live-OS / install-media disk is always skipped. In batch mode, check the servers you want to wipe; only the focused server’s table is shown (list stays fast at 1000+ hosts).

Scan to list disks and partitions on the live OS.
